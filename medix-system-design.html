Medix — system & design specification
Foundation

Medix

A pharmaceutical commerce, operations, compliance and intelligence platform for Rwanda — built as one transaction ledger with four windows cut into it.

A Rwandan pharmacy runs on disconnected paper. Purchases in one notebook, sales in another, expiry in a third, insurance in a fourth. At night the pharmacist reconciles by hand and gets it roughly right. The owner cannot answer did we make money — they know revenue, guess at margin, and value stock from a count taken weeks ago.

Two authorities want data from that same counter. Rwanda FDA asks whether the product is registered, stored and dispensed lawfully. RRA asks whether every sale produced a fiscal invoice. The pharmacy does the same work five times and still can't answer the two questions that matter.

The thesis. Medix is not five digital notebooks. It is one transaction ledger with five views. Every screen is a lens on a segment of a single chain — and if any link is bypassable, the chain rots and the notebooks come back.

The spine

Product→Listing→Batch→Inventory→Purchase→Order→Shipment→Goods receipt→Stock→Prescription→Sale→Claim→EBM invoice→Financial record→Report

The pharmacist looks at the middle. The owner looks at the end. The wholesaler looks at the beginning. The regulator looks across all of it. Same data, different windows.

Foundation

The four decisions everything depends on

Get these wrong and the product is rebuilt, not patched. Each has to be made before the first screen is written.

01 — Stock is a ledger, never a counter

stock = stock - 1 kills the system. Every movement is an immutable row referencing why. Recall answers in seconds, margin knows the batch cost, disputes settle by replay, and audit comes free.

02 — Allocation is FEFO

First Expired, First Out — not FIFO. The system auto-selects the nearest-expiry batch with stock. Override is allowed, requires a reason, and is logged. FIFO silently generates write-offs.

03 — Unit-of-measure hierarchy

This market sells six tablets, not a box of a hundred. Carton → pack → blister → tablet, all reconciling against one batch. Purchasing in packs, dispensing in units. Unfixable later.

04 — Cloud plus local agent

RRA's VSDC runs on the taxpayer's own local server, so a pure cloud SaaS cannot issue fiscal invoices. The same agent is what keeps POS working offline. Two requirements, one component.

Four products over one ledger

Not role-based hiding of a single dashboard. Genuinely different applications sharing a database.

Progressive disclosure is the mechanism. A one-branch owner sees Sell, Order, Stock, Reports — and never learns RFQ, GRN or ASN. Those surface when the business grows into them. Complexity lives underneath the interface, not in front of the user.

The interaction model

Search→Table→Detail drawer→Full transaction

Drawers for preview, quick edit and activity — they keep the user in context. Full pages only for genuine workflows: purchase order creation, import request, receiving, POS, prescription processing, insurance claim. This is what makes an ERP fast rather than click-heavy.

Product type changes the card

A medicine, a cosmetic, a device and a consumable should not be forced into an identical card. A medicine needs generic name, strength, dosage form and prescription status; a device needs model, specification and sterility; a cosmetic needs brand, size and use. This is why the product model needs dynamic attributes per type, not one flat table.

Three things the POS must get right

  • Prescription gating blocks, it does not warn. A prescription-only product stops the sale. OCR may extract fields; OCR never authorizes. A registered pharmacist confirms, and their council number attaches to the dispensing event.
  • Payment is asynchronous. Mobile money uses request-to-pay with a callback — the customer confirms on their phone. The sale needs a pending state that may resolve in seconds, time out, or require reconciliation. The three buttons above are not three equivalent instant actions.
  • Tax is per line. Medicines are VAT-exempt; cosmetics, devices and consumables generally are not. Every invoice is mixed-treatment, and exempt is not zero-rated — input VAT is not reclaimable, which changes the true cost basis and therefore margin.

Why this is the defensible piece. A pharmacy needing 75 packs cannot import — it fails MOQ and per-unit clearance makes it uneconomic. Twenty pharmacies clear it easily. No existing actor can see that aggregate demand exists, because it is scattered across twenty separate phone calls. Medix makes previously impossible imports possible — that is the adoption argument, not "nicer software".

Four problems consolidation creates

  • Partial-arrival allocation. 425 ordered, 400 arrive — who gets short? Needs a stated policy per order, and one consignment must produce separate goods receipts per pharmacy with correct batch traceability.
  • Landed-cost apportionment. Shipping and clearance are charged to the shipment, not the product. They must be apportioned across participants to produce the batch cost basis — which drives every margin number in the executive portal. Highest-leverage detail in the module.
  • FX exposure. Sourced in USD, paid in RWF, 21–45 days apart. Record quote currency, rate, rate date, and whether the quote is fixed or indicative.
  • Commitment. One withdrawal can drop the group below MOQ and break it for everyone. Needs a binding commitment point, probably with a deposit.

Domain

The stock ledger

The single technical decision everything else rests on. Stock is never a number you edit.

DATE     EVENT        IN    OUT   BALANCE   REFERENCE
11 Aug   Opening                  500       MIG-0001
11 Aug   Purchase     200         700       GRN-00412 · batch AMX-2601
11 Aug   Sale               20    680       SAL-00982 · till 02 · Olivier
11 Aug   Transfer           50    630       TRF-0088 → Remera
11 Aug   Adjustment          2    628       Damage · approved Marie
  • Recall must answer "who received units from AMX-2601" in seconds. A counter cannot.
  • Margin needs batch-level cost. You bought at 28,000 and at 31,000 — which did you just sell?
  • Disputes settle by replay: cash short at day end, claim rejected, count mismatch.
  • Audit comes free. You do not build auditing as a separate feature — the ledger is the audit.

Compliance obligations found in research, absent from the original design

  • Law 058/2021 — health data is sensitive personal data. Consent, NCSA registration as data controller, a designated data protection officer, retention policy, subject access and erasure, and cross-border transfer limits that constrain hosting region.
  • Law 03/2012 — every delivery of a narcotic or psychotropic must be registered immediately on the prescription with the patient's name and address. A statutory register, not a classification flag.
  • Pharmacist registration — dispensing must be attributable to a professional registered with the National Pharmacy Council, with council number and licence expiry. Premises licensing is separate and carries facility standards.
  • Cold chain — insulin and vaccines require 2–8°C. The running example product in the original specification was insulin, shipped internationally over 21–45 days, with no temperature concept anywhere in the design.
  • GS1 DataMatrix — packs encode GTIN, expiry, batch and serial. Parsing them means a scan auto-fills batch and expiry, removing most manual entry in the system. Confirm the mandate dates that apply to Rwanda.
Delivery

Open decisions

Everything else in this document is settled. These four are not, and two of them change what gets built first.

01
Dark theme

Proposed above and used by this page's toggle. Worth confirming as in-scope, since the eleven-at-night scenario in the specification implies night shifts. The token structure supports it cleanly because every surface is already a named level.

02
Monetisation

Subscription, transaction fee on marketplace and import, or both. Determines whether the wholesaler portal is a feature or the actual product.

03
Stack

React and Tailwind are assumed from the source material. Backend unspecified. Hosting region constrained by the data-protection finding above.

04
Build sequence

Design system, then module templates, then screens — never screen by screen. Pharmacy-first, because demand consolidation needs pharmacy density before importers care.
